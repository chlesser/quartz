#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Platoon:** If at least 3 other UNSC allies are within 40 feet of the UNSC (ODST), they can make 1 additional attack with a weapon of medium size or smaller.

_(Passive)_ **UNSC Expertise:** Add +2 to all attacks made with UNSC weapons.

_(Operational)_ **Reactive:** As a Reactive Operation, the UNSC (ODST) can add +4 (1d6) to a dodge roll.
