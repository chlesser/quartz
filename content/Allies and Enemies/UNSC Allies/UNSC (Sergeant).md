#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Platoon:** If at least 3 other UNSC allies are within 40 feet of the UNSC (Sergeant), they can make 1 additional attack with a weapon of medium size or smaller.

_(Passive)_ **Efficient:** Add +3 to all attacks made with the Battle Rifle.

_(Operational)_ **Rally:** As a Primary Operation the UNSC (Sergeant) can roll a d20. All allies within 20 feet regain an amount of health equal to that roll.
