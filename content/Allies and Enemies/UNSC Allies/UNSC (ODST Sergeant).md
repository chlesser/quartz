#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Platoon:** If at least 3 other UNSC allies are within 40 feet of the UNSC (ODST Sergeant), they can make 1 additional attack with a weapon of medium size or smaller.

_(Passive)_ **Efficient:** Add +3 to all attacks made with the Battle Rifle.

_(Operational)_ **Rally:** As a Primary Operation the UNSC (ODST Sergeant) can roll a d20. All allies within 25 feet regain an amount of health equal to that roll.

_(Operational)_ **Inspire:** As a Secondary and Tertiary Operation, the ODST sergeant can select 1 ally within 30 feet and roll 1d6. The damage of all attacks made by that ally is increased by the amount rolled until the end of that ally’s next turn.
