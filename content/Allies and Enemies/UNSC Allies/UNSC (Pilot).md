#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Platoon:** If at least 3 other UNSC allies are within 40 feet of the UNSC (Pilot), they can make 1 additional attack with a weapon of medium size or smaller.

_(Passive)_ **Vehicle Expertise:** Add +5 Dodge Bonus while operating vehicles.

_(Operational)_ **Flight Proficiency:** As a Tertiary Operation while operating a vehicle, the UNSC (Pilot) can roll a d4 and multiply the amount rolled by 5 and add the total to the vehicle's movement speed for that turn.
