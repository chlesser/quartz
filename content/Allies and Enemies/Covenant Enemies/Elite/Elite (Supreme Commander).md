#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Nightvision:** The Elite (Supreme Commander) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness.

_(Innate)_ **Honor In Death:** When the Elite (Supreme Commander) is reduced to 0 health, it may roll a d100. If the result of this roll is above 40, the Elite (Supreme Commander) is not killed and drops to 1 health. Each time it uses this ability the DC increases by 20.

_(Innate)_ **Brotherhood of Warriors:** If an Elite is killed within sight of an Elite (Supreme Commander), the Elite (Supreme Commander) may immediately make a single attack targeted on the creature that killed the Elite, provided they are within range and within line of sight.

_(Passive)_ **Leader:** When three or more Elites are conscious within 50 feet of the Elite (Supreme Commander), the Elite (Supreme Commander) gains an additional +10 dodge and may make an additional attack with their weapon

_(Passive)_ **Commanding Presence:** Allies within 15 feet gain an additional \+5 Dodge Bonus, excluding the Elite (Supreme Commander) itself.

_(Passive)_ **Battle Tactics:** On their turn, the Elite (Supreme Commander) can sacrifice up to half their movement speed (rounded down) in feet and choose an allied creature that can see and hear them. On the target creature’s next turn, their movement speed is increased by the amount of feet sacrificed by the Elite (Supreme Commander) until the end of their turn. 

_(Passive)_ **Inspiring Presence:** Any allies within 15 feet of the Elite (Supreme Commander) are immune to all negative effects presented by any battlefield externalities.

_(Passive)_ **Unwavering Presence:** Any allies that can see the Elite (Supreme Commander) are immune to the Frightened effect.

_(Passive)_ **Reliant:** When the Elite (Supreme Commander) rolls a 1 on a dodge, they suffer no adverse effects other than failing the dodge. 

_(Passive)_ **Menacing:** Enemy creatures within 20 feet of the Elite (Supreme Commander) must make a DC 55 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Frightened effect. Each creature affected by this can remake the Fortitude interaction roll at the end of each turn. A creature that succeeds its interaction roll becomes immune to this effect for 24 hours.

_(Operational)_ **Veteran Tactician:** As a Reactive Operation, the Elite (Supreme Commander) can choose one creature to reroll a dodge.
