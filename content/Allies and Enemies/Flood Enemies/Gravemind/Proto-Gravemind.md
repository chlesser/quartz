#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Enhanced Hive Mind:** The Dodge DC of the Proto-Gravemind’s attacks increase by +10 for each Flood creature within 15 feet of it, along with its Dodge Bonus and interactions that it has Superiority or Expertise in.

_(Passive)_ **Flammable:** The Proto-Gravemind takes double damage from Thermal damage.

_(Passive)_ **Immobile:** The Proto-Gravemind is incapable of moving any part of itself to douse flames, make melee attacks, or any other Operations requiring movements.

_(Passive)_ **Hive Mind Nexus:** Any Flood creatures within 60 feet of the Proto-Gravemind gain an additional +20 bonus to their Dodge DCs, dodge Bonus, and movement speed, as well as regaining 11 (2d10) health at the start of each of their turns.

_(Passive)_ **Menacing:** Enemy creatures within 60 feet of the Proto-Gravemind must make a DC 85 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Frightened effect. Each creature affected by this can remake the Fortitude interaction roll at the end of each turn. A creature that succeeds its interaction roll becomes immune to this effect for 24 hours.

_(Passive)_ **Miasma:** Non-Flood Creatures race who start their turn within 10 feet of the Proto-Gravemind are exposed to the Blinded effect for as long as they are within 10 feet of the Proto-Gravemind.

_(Passive)_ **Blindsight:** The Proto-Gravemind can detect any creature within 60 feet of it without any interaction roll, regardless of an invisibility or any form of obscurity.

_(Operational)_ **Biomass:** As a Secondary Operation, the Proto-Gravemind can create 6 (1d10) Infection Forms or 3 (1d4) Stalker Forms in an unoccupied space within 10 feet. Any creatures created in this way die when the Proto-Gravemind that created them dies.

_(Operational)_ **Poison:** As a Primary Operation, the Proto-Gravemind can pick a point within 30 feet, forcing all creatures within 5 feet to make a DC 80 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Poisoned effect. Any creatures already exposed to the Poisoned effect take an additional 28 (6d10) Acid damage.

_(Operational)_ **Empower:** As a Primary Operation, the Proto-Gravemind can select 1 Flood creature it can see within 30 feet and expose them to the Indomitable effect until the end of their next turn.
