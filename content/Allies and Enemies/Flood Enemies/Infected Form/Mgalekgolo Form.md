#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Hive Mind:** The Dodge DC of the Mgalekgolo Form’s attacks increase by +5 for each Flood creature within 10 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Virus:** When a target is hit by a melee attack from the Mgalekgolo Form, they must make a DC 50 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Poisoned effect.

_(Passive)_ **Flammable:** Mgalekgolo Forms take double damage from Thermal damage.

_(Passive)_ **Spasm:** Mgalekgolo Forms may use the Dash, Grapple, and Knockdown maneuvers.

_(Passive)_ **Unique Weapon:** The Mgalekgolo Form uses the Assault Cannon attached to its arm to make all weapon attacks.

_(Passive)_ **Shielded:** The Mgalekgolo Form has a +20 to all dodges from attackers that it can see. If the attack would otherwise hit, it hits the shield. The shield has 500 health.

_(Passive)_ **Armored:** All damage dealt to the Mgalekgolo Form is halved (rounded down) unless the Mgalekgolo Form fails the dodge by 40 or more, is dealt Explosive damage or is exposed to the Surprised effect.

_(Operational)_ **Spring:** Instead of moving normally, the Mgalekgolo Form can jump up to 25 feet in any direction from a standing position.

_(Operational)_ **Clobber:** The Mgalekgolo Form may spend a Secondary Operation to make a melee attack.
