#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Hive Mind:** The Dodge DC of the Ung’goy Form’s attacks increase by +5 for each Flood creature within 10 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Virus:** When a target is hit by a melee attack from the Ung’goy Form, they must make a DC 50 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Poisoned effect.

_(Passive)_ **Flammable:** Ung’goy Forms take double damage from Thermal damage.

_(Passive)_ **Spasm:** Ung’goy Forms may use the Dash, Grapple, and Knockdown maneuvers.

_(Passive)_ **Weapon Inefficiency:** The maximum amount of weapon attacks the Ung’goy Form can make in a turn is halved (rounded down).

_(Operational)_ **Spring:** Instead of moving normally, the Ung’goy Form can jump up to 30 feet in any direction from a standing position.

_(Operational)_ **Clobber:** The Ung’goy Form may spend a Secondary Operation to make a melee attack.
