#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Hive Mind:** The Dodge DC of the Jiralhanae Form’s attacks increase by +5 for each Flood creature within 10 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Virus:** When a target is hit by a melee attack from the Jiralhanae Form, they must make a DC 50 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Poisoned effect.

_(Passive)_ **Flammable:** Jiralhanae Forms take double damage from Thermal damage.

_(Passive)_ **Spasm:** Jiralhanae Forms may use the Dash, Grapple, and Knockdown maneuvers.

_(Passive)_ **Weapon Inefficiency:** The maximum amount of weapon attacks the Jiralhanae Form can make in a turn is halved (rounded down).

_(Operational)_ **Spring:** Instead of moving normally, the Jiralhanae Form can jump up to 45 feet in any direction from a standing position.

_(Operational)_ **Clobber:** The Jiralhanae Form may spend a Secondary Operation to make a melee attack.

_(Operational)_ **Grab:** If the Jiralhanae Form hits a target with a melee attack, they may immediately take the Grapple combat maneuver, expending only a Secondary and Tertiary Operation.
