#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Hive Mind:** The Dodge DC of the Yanme’e Form’s attacks increase by +5 for each Flood creature within 10 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Virus:** When a target is hit by a melee attack from the Yanme’e Form, they must make a DC 50 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Poisoned effect.

_(Passive)_ **Flammable:** Yanme’e Forms take double damage from Thermal damage.

_(Passive)_ **Spasm:** Yanme’e Forms may use the Dash, Grapple, and Knockdown maneuvers.

_(Passive)_ **Weapon Inefficiency:** The maximum amount of weapon attacks the Yanme’e Form can make in a turn is halved (rounded down).

_(Operational)_ **Clobber:** The Yanme’e Form may spend a Secondary Operation to make a melee attack.
