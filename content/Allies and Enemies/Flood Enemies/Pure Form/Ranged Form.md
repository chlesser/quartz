#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Hive Mind:** The Dodge DC of the Ranged Form’s attacks increase by +5 for each Flood creature within 10 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Virus:** When a target is hit by a melee attack from the Ranged Form, they must make a DC 50 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Poisoned effect.

_(Passive)_ **Flammable:** Ranged Forms take double damage from Thermal damage.

_(Passive)_ **Spasm:** Ranged Forms may use the Dash, Grapple, and Knockdown maneuvers.

_(Passive)_ **Skitter:** The Ranged Form can move freely on walls and ceilings without penalty.

_(Passive)_ **Unique Weapon:** The Ranged Form uses the Spikes attached to its body to make all attacks.

_(Operational)_ **Spring:** Instead of moving normally, the Ranged Form can jump up to 30 feet in any direction from a standing position.

_(Operational)_ **Clobber:** The Ranged Form may spend a Secondary Operation to make a melee attack.
