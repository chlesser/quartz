#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Enhanced Hive Mind:** The Dodge DC of the Juggernaut Form’s attacks increase by +10 for each Flood creature within 15 feet of it, along with its Dodge Bonus and interactions that it has Superiority or Expertise in.

_(Passive)_ **Virus:** When a target is hit by a melee attack from the Juggernaut Form, they must make a DC 70 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Poisoned effect.

_(Passive)_ **Flammable:** Juggernaut Forms take double damage from Thermal damage.

_(Passive)_ **Spasm:** Juggernaut Forms may use the Dash, Grapple, and Knockdown maneuvers.

_(Passive)_ **Reach:** The Juggernaut Form can make melee attacks against creatures within 15 feet.

_(Operational)_ **Spring:** Instead of moving normally, the Juggernaut Form can jump up to 20 feet in any direction from a standing position.

_(Operational)_ **Clobber:** The Juggernaut Form may spend a Tertiary Operation to make a melee attack.

_(Operational)_ **Biomass:** As a Secondary Operation, the Juggernaut can create 4 (1d6) Infection Forms or 1 Stalker Form in an unoccupied space within 10 feet. Any creatures created in this way die when the Juggernaut that created them dies.

_(Operational)_ **Disrupt:** As a Primary Operation, the Juggernaut Form can force all creatures within melee range to make a DC 75 Agility interaction or be exposed to the Fallen effect and take 17 (3d10) Kinetic damage.

_(Operational)_ **Engulf:** As a Tertiary Operation, the Juggernaut Form may make a melee attack against a creature exposed to the Fallen effect. On a hit, the target creature is engulfed into the innards of the Juggernaut form, taking 55 (10d10) Acid damage at the start of their turn. Additionally, the creature is exposed to the Blinded and Incapacitated effects as well as having their speed reduced to 0. Only one creature at a time can be engulfed in this way. While engulfed, the creature cannot be targeted by any attacks made from outside the Juggernaut Form. If the Juggernaut Form takes 50 or more damage from a single attack, they must make a DC 90 Fortitude interaction or spit out the engulfed creature anywhere within 10 feet, exposing it to the Fallen effect.
