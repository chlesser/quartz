#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Hive Mind:** The Dodge DC of the Infection Form’s attacks increase by +5 for each Flood creature within 10 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Virus:** When a target is hit by a melee attack from the Infection Form, they must make a DC 50 Fortitude interaction or be exposed to the Poisoned effect.

_(Passive)_ **Flammable:** Infection Forms take double damage from Thermal damage.

_(Passive)_ **Skitter:** The Infection Form can move freely on walls and ceilings without penalty.

_(Operational)_ **Infection:** When a creature that is exposed to the Poisoned effect is hit by a melee attack from the Infection Form, the target must succeed on a DC 10 Fortitude interaction. Each time the target is hit by an Infection Form’s melee attack, the DC of the Fortitude interaction increases by +5. On a failure, the target dies and transforms into the Flood form of their race in 3 (1d4) turns. The Infection Form dies if it lands a melee attack.

_(Operational)_ **Spring:** Instead of moving normally, the Infection Form can jump up to 30 feet in any direction from a standing position.
