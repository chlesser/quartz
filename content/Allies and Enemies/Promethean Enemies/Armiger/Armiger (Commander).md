#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Promethean Vision:** The Armiger (Commander) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

_(Passive)_ **Blink:** After making a successful dodge roll against an attacker they can see, the Armiger (Commander) may use their Reactive Operation to instantly teleport to any unoccupied space that they can see within 30 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Shutdown:** While within 35 feet of a Soldier (Commander), hostile creatures cannot regenerate shields.

_(Passive)_ **Chain Attack:** The first time a Soldier (Commander) hits a creature with an attack on their turn, they may choose 1 Promethean Ally within 40 feet to make a single weapon attack against the same target.
