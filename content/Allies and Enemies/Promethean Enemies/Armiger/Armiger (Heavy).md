#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Promethean Vision:** The Armiger (Heavy) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

_(Passive)_ **Blink:** After making a successful dodge roll against an attacker they can see, the Armiger (Heavy) may use their Reactive Operation to instantly teleport to any unoccupied space that they can see within 30 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Resilient:** The Armiger (Heavy) can take the Brace Maneuver using only a Tertiary Operation.

_(Passive)_ **Suppressing Fire:** When the Soldier (Heavy) misses a weapon attack they may make a single additional weapon attack on a different creature within range.

_(Passive)_ **Reductive Armoring:** Plasma damage deals no additional damage die to the Soldier (Heavy)’s shields.
