#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Promethean Vision:** The Armiger (Scout) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

_(Passive)_ **Improved Blink:** After making a successful dodge roll against an attacker they can see, the Armiger (Scout) may use their Reactive Operation to instantly teleport to any unoccupied space within 45 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Maneuverable:** The Armiger (Scout) has a +25 Dodge Bonus against Chance Attacks.

_(Operational)_ **Limited Invisibility:** As a Primary Operation, the Soldier (Scout) can expose themselves to the Invisible effect, which lasts until they make an attack, end the effect as a Tertiary Operation, or until the end of their next turn.
