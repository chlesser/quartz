#### Skills
_(Innate)_ **Promethean Vision:** The Armiger (Standard) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

_(Passive)_ **Blink:** After making a successful dodge roll against an attacker they can see, the Armiger (Standard) may use their Reactive Operation to instantly teleport to any unoccupied space that they can see within 30 feet of it.

_(Passive)_ **Maneuverable:** The Armiger (Standard) has a +25 Dodge Bonus against Chance Attacks.
