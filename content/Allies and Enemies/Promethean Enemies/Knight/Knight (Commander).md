#### Skills
*(Innate)* **Promethean Vision:** The Knight (Commander) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

*(Innate)* **Teleport:** As a Secondary and Tertiary Operation, the Knight (Commander) can teleport to an unoccupied space within 30 of it.

*(Innate)* **Scream:** As a Secondary Operation, the Knight (Commander) can open its helmet and scream, forcing up to 3 creatures within 20 feet to make a DC 55 Fortitude interaction or become exposed to the Frightened effect until the end of the Knight (Commander)’s next turn. If the target succeeds their Fortitude interaction they become immune to this effect for 24 hours.

*(Passive)* **Nexus:** While within 50 feet of the Knight (Commander) all Promethean allies gain a +5 dodge bonus and an additional Tertiary Operation.

*(Passive)* **Forceful Blow:** When the Knight (Commander) hits a creature with an attack that creature must make a DC 55 Fortitude check or be pushed up to 15 feet back. This can be negated with active anti-gravity armor.

*(Operational)* **Autosentry:** The Knight (Commander) can spend a Primary Operation to deploy an autosentry in an unoccupied space anywhere within 5 feet of them. However, when doing so the Knight (Commander) loses all movement speed for that turn. The autosentry’s stat block is below.
