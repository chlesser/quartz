#### Skills
*(Innate)* **Promethean Vision:** The Knight (Battlewagon) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

*(Innate)* **Teleport:** As a Secondary and Tertiary Operation, the Knight (Commander) can teleport to an unoccupied space within 30 of it.

*(Innate)* **Scream:** As a Secondary Operation, the Knight (Commander) can open its helmet and scream, forcing up to 3 creatures within 20 feet to make a DC 60 Fortitude interaction or become exposed to the Frightened effect until the end of the Knight (Commander)’s next turn. If the target succeeds their Fortitude interaction they become immune to this effect for 24 hours.

*(Passive)* **Reductive Armoring:** Plasma damage deals no additional damage die to the Knight (Battlewagon)’s shields.

*(Passive)* **Disintegrate:** Enemies reduced to 0 health by the Knight (Battlewagon) die instantly. Their bodies as well as anything they are wearing or carrying disintegrates and is permanently destroyed.

*(Operational)* **Hardlight Absorption:** As a Reactive Operation when hit by an attack, the Knight (Battlewagon) can choose to ignore all damage received from a single attack as long as they have shields remaining and can see their attacker.
