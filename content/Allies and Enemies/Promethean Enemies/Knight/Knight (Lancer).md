#### Skills
*(Innate)* **Promethean Vision:** The Knight (Lancer) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

*(Innate)* **Teleport:** As a Secondary and Tertiary Operation, the Knight (Lancer) can teleport to an unoccupied space within 30 of it.

*(Innate)* **Scream:** As a Secondary Operation, the Knight (Lancer) can open its helmet and scream, forcing up to 3 creatures within 20 feet to make a DC 55 Fortitude interaction or become exposed to the Frightened effect until the end of the Knight (Lancer)’s next turn. If the target succeeds their Fortitude interaction they become immune to this effect for 24 hours.

*(Passive)* **Stationary Concealment:** If the Knight (Lancer) has not moved since the beginning of its last turn, then it becomes exposed to the Invisible effect, which ends once it moves.

*(Passive)* **Siphon:** When the Knight (Lancer) deals damage to an target’s shields, their own shields are restored by a number equal to a quarter of the damage dealt rounded down.

*(Passive)* **Penetrate:** When the Knight (Lancer) lands an attack with a precision weapon, if the target has shields the Knight (Lancer) can use up to half of the weapon’s damage die (rounded down) to ignore shields and damage the target’s health.
