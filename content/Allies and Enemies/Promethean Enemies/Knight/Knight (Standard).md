#### Skills
*(Innate)* **Promethean Vision:** The Knight (Standard) ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

*(Innate)* **Teleport:** As a Secondary and Tertiary Operation, the Knight (Standard) can teleport to an unoccupied space within 30 of it.

*(Innate)* **Scream:** As a Secondary Operation, the Knight (Standard) can open its helmet and scream, forcing up to 3 creatures within 20 feet to make a DC 55 Fortitude interaction or become exposed to the Frightened effect until the end of the Knight (Standard)’s next turn. If the target succeeds their Fortitude interaction they become immune to this effect for 24 hours.

*(Passive)* **Strike:** The Knight (Standard) may use the Dash maneuver and if they end their movement within 5 feet of an enemy they may make a Melee attack using no Operation.
