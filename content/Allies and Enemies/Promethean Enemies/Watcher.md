#### Skills
*(Innate)* **Promethean* Vision:** The Watcher ignores penalties resulting from Darkness, and can see any creature within 60 feet that is not obscured by cover.

*(Passive)* **Weapon Inefficiency:** The maximum amount of weapon attacks the Watcher can make in a turn is halved (rounded down).

*(Passive)* **Space Effectiveness:** The Watcher can ignore movement speed penalties while in space.

*(Operational)* **Revival:** If a watcher remains within 10 feet of a dead Promethean for 2 consecutive rounds without taking any other Operations, then at the start of the Watcher’s third turn the target Promethean is revived with half of its maximum health (rounded down) and full shields.

*(Operational)* **Hardlight Shield:** As a Primary Operation the Watcher can create a hardlight shield surrounding a Promethean ally, granting it Average Cover. The shield has 100 shield health and a Dodge Bonus equal to the affected creature’s Dodge Bonus. and disappears if the Watcher is killed
